About GA4 auto-detection

After DNS verification, TrafficGen reads the website's initial HTML and looks for Google tag Measurement IDs (G-…) and Google Tag Manager containers (GTM-…). Found IDs prefill the campaign GA4 field, but detection does not prove that Google Analytics is receiving events. Tags loaded dynamically or after consent may not appear in the initial HTML.
